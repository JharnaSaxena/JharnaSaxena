## 💼 Experience

**🧮 HPC / Computational Physics Research Intern** · May 2026 – Aug 2026
- Built a hybrid numerical framework for the Lüscher zeta function Z₀₀ (Chebyshev-sampled Padé approximants, Ewald resummation, two-level LRU caching): **2200× per-call speedup**.
- Designed an adaptive branch-tracking root-finder that removed spurious energy branches, raising scattering-fit convergence from **10% to 100%** (37/45 levels matched to published spectra).
- Shipped a Python/C++ pipeline with HDF5 and covariance-aware χ² minimization: **981 s → 85 s (11.5×)** through memoization, matrix caching, and parallelization. Documented in a public technical report on GitHub.

**🔬 Research Assistant, Lanczos Methods for Lattice QCD Spectroscopy** · 2026 – Present
- Writing a from-scratch correlated multi-exponential fitter (covariance matrices, jackknife/bootstrap, GEVP) to reproduce published spectra, then extending it to Lanczos and block-Lanczos estimators.

**🤖 Backend AI Engineer Intern, FlyRank AI** · Jun 2026 – Present
- Building backend services and AI features for the platform.

**⚖️ Research Affiliate, Algorithmic Fairness, UC Berkeley (REYES)** · Jul 2025 – Dec 2025
- Led an independent study of bias in e-commerce review and ranking systems; proposed fairness-aware recommendation mechanisms. Report approved, certificate awarded.
